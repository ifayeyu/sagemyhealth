About Us

Health answers that actually
make sense

SageMyHealth exists because health information should be accessible, honest, and human — not buried in clinical language or muddied by sponsored results.

We believe that understanding your own body is a right, not a privilege.

Most health calculators online are either too clinical to be useful, too ad-heavy to be trusted, or too vague to be actionable. SageMyHealth was built as a direct response to that — a space where anyone can get a clear, evidence-based answer to a real question about their health, without having to wade through pop-ups, sponsored content, or medical jargon.

Every calculator on this site is built on established scientific formulas — the same ones used by clinicians, researchers, and health professionals. We explain what the formulas are, where they come from, and what the results mean in plain language. Not dumbed down. Just human.

We cover the full spectrum of health — body composition, cardiovascular fitness, nutrition, sleep, mental wellbeing, women's health, respiratory health, and more. Because health isn't one thing. It's everything, all at once, and you deserve tools that reflect that.

Our principles

Built on three things we won't compromise

🔬

Evidence-based formulas

Every calculation on SageMyHealth uses established medical and scientific formulas — Mifflin-St Jeor, Knudson, Harris-Benedict, NSF guidelines, and more. We cite our sources, we explain the science, and we never invent our own numbers. If a formula has been validated by research, we use it. If it hasn't, we don't.

🔒

Complete privacy by design

We don't store your health data. Ever. Every calculation you run happens entirely within your own browser — nothing is sent to a server, logged to a database, or associated with your identity. Health information is personal. We treat it that way. No accounts, no tracking, no profiles built from your inputs.

💬

Plain language, always

A result you don't understand isn't a result. Every calculator on this site explains what your number means, what the research says, and what (if anything) you might consider doing about it. Warm, direct, and never condescending — like a knowledgeable friend who happens to know the science.

How SageMyHealth works

There's nothing complicated under the hood. Our calculators are self-contained tools — no accounts, no subscriptions, no apps to download. Open a calculator, enter your information, get your answer. That's it.

We've designed each calculator to give you three things: a clear result, the context to understand it, and related tools if you want to go deeper.

Our commitments

What you can always expect from us

🚫

No sponsored health advice

Our calculator results and health guidance are never influenced by sponsors or advertisers. We will never change what a formula tells you based on who's paying. Any future commercial partnerships will always be clearly disclosed and kept separate from our health tools.

🌿

Health-only focus

This site covers health and nothing else. No finance calculators, no random tools, no pivot to lifestyle content. Everything here is about your body and wellbeing — nothing else.

📖

Transparent sources

Every calculator includes an About section that explains the formula used, where it comes from, and its known limitations. We show our working.

⚕️

Honest about our limits

Calculators are tools, not diagnoses. We always tell you when a result warrants professional attention, and we never overstate what a formula can tell you.

♾️

Always free

Every calculator on SageMyHealth is free, and will remain free. Access to health information should not depend on ability to pay.

🔄

Kept up to date

Medical guidelines evolve. When the evidence changes, our calculators change with it. We review and update formulas when clinical guidance is updated.
