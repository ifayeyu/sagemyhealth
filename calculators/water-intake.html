Tips to hit your target

    Why your exact number matters

    Generic advice says "8 glasses a day" — but that ignores body size entirely. A 50 kg woman and a 100 kg man have very different fluid needs. The weight-based formula (≈33 ml per kg) is what most clinical guidelines use as a starting point, adjusted for age, sex, activity, and environment.

    About 20% of daily fluid intake comes from food (fruits, vegetables, soups). This calculator gives you a pure drinking target — the water you should consume as liquid, separately from meals.

    Recognise dehydration before it hits hard

    Thirst is a late signal — by the time you feel thirsty, you're often already 1–2% dehydrated, enough to impair concentration and physical performance. Watch for earlier cues:

    Dark yellow urine
    Dry mouth or lips
    Difficulty concentrating
    Low energy / fatigue
    Headache
    Decreased urination
    Dizziness
    Muscle cramps
    Rapid heartbeat
    Confusion
    Sunken eyes
    No urination (8+ hrs)
