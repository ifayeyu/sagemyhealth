    About the GAD-7

    The GAD-7 is a seven-item self-report questionnaire that screens for and measures the severity of generalized anxiety disorder. It was developed by researchers at Columbia University and has been validated in numerous studies. A score of 10 or higher suggests clinically significant anxiety. This tool is not a diagnosis—it's a starting point for conversations with a healthcare provider.
