About lung capacity

Lung capacity is the maximum amount of air your lungs can hold. The two key clinical measurements are FVC (Forced Vital Capacity — total air exhaled after a full breath) and FEV1 (Forced Expiratory Volume in 1 second — air exhaled in the first second). Together they reveal both the volume and efficiency of your airways. This calculator uses the Knudson reference equations — the same population-based equations used in clinical spirometry to estimate expected values for your age, height, and sex.
