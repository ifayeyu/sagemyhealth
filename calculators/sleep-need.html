About sleep need

Sleep need is the total amount of sleep your body requires to function optimally — and it's largely biological, not a matter of willpower. The National Sleep Foundation sets guidelines by age: adults need 7–9 hours, teens 8–10, school-age children 9–12, and older adults 7–8. But your personal need can shift based on physical activity, stress load, health status, and accumulated sleep debt. Chronically sleeping even 1–2 hours less than you need leads to cumulative impairment in memory, mood, reaction time, and immune function.
