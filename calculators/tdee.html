A few things worth knowing:

    What is TDEE?

    Total Daily Energy Expenditure is the total number of calories your body burns each day — factoring in your resting metabolism, the energy used to digest food, and your physical activity. It's calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the most widely validated BMR formula in clinical use today.
