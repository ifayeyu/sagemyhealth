About iron deficiency

Iron deficiency is the most common nutritional deficiency worldwide, affecting roughly 25% of the global population. The key marker is ferritin — the protein that stores iron in your body. You can have depleted ferritin stores (and experience symptoms like fatigue, brain fog, and hair loss) even when a basic haemoglobin test comes back "normal." Groups at highest risk include menstruating women, vegetarians and vegans, athletes, and those with GI conditions. A simple blood test checking serum ferritin is the definitive way to check.
