Note: Brain fatigue is cumulative — it builds over days and weeks. If you've been running on empty for a while, recovery takes intentional rest, not just a single good night's sleep.

What is brain fatigue?

Mental fatigue is different from physical tiredness — it's the cognitive and emotional exhaustion that comes from sustained mental effort, poor sleep, chronic stress, and sensory overload. Unlike physical fatigue, it doesn't resolve with a single rest. It's cumulative: it builds over days and weeks when the mental load consistently outpaces recovery. This assessment scores five key domains — sleep quality, cognitive load, stress levels, lifestyle habits, and active symptoms — to give you an overall picture of your current mental energy state.
