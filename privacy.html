Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Effective date: June 2025

The short version: SageMyHealth does not collect, store, or sell your health data. Every calculation you run happens entirely within your browser. We don't know what you entered, what your result was, or who you are. That's by design.

1. Who we are

SageMyHealth ("we", "us", "our") operates the website located at sagemyhealth.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the site. If you have questions, you can reach us at hello@sagemyhealth.com.

2. Information we do not collect

SageMyHealth calculators are built to process your inputs locally in your browser. We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Any health data you enter into our calculators (weight, age, blood glucose readings, symptoms, medications, etc.)
  • Your calculator results or health scores
  • Your name, email address, or any other personal information (unless you contact us directly)
  • Account information (we have no accounts or login system)

When you close or navigate away from a calculator page, your inputs are gone. We have no record of them.

3. Information that may be collected automatically

Like virtually all websites, some technical information may be collected automatically when you visit SageMyHealth. This includes:

Web server logs

Our hosting provider may log standard web server data such as your IP address, browser type, the page you visited, and the date and time of your visit. This information is used solely for technical operation and security — not for identifying individuals or profiling visitors.

Google Fonts

SageMyHealth uses Google Fonts, a free typography service provided by Google LLC. When a page loads, your browser makes a request to Google's servers to download the fonts used on our site. Google may log this request, which can include your IP address and browser information. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We use Google Fonts solely for visual presentation of the site.

Analytics (if enabled)

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns — for example, which calculators are most used. If analytics are implemented, they are configured to anonymize IP addresses and avoid cross-site tracking. We do not use Google Analytics' advertising features or share analytics data with third parties.

Cookies

SageMyHealth does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or cookies that build a profile of your behavior. Your browser may store basic session data (such as your preference settings on a calculator) locally, but this data never leaves your device.

4. Information you send to us directly

If you contact us by email (for example, to report an error or ask a question), we will receive and store your email address and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to you. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent, and we do not share your contact information with third parties.

5. Third-party links

Some pages on SageMyHealth contain links to third-party websites, including our Health Guides section which links to external articles. When you click a link to another site, you leave SageMyHealth and that site's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party sites, and we encourage you to review their policies before providing any personal information.

6. Children's privacy

SageMyHealth is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hello@sagemyhealth.com and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

7. Your rights under US privacy law (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) provides you with certain rights regarding your personal information. Because we collect very limited personal information, most of these rights have limited practical application to your use of SageMyHealth — but we honor them in full.

Right to knowYou can request information about the personal data we have collected about you.
Right to deleteYou can request that we delete personal information we have collected from you.
Right to opt outWe do not sell personal information. There is nothing to opt out of.
Right to non-discriminationWe will not treat you differently for exercising any privacy right.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@sagemyhealth.com. We will respond within 45 days.

8. Data security

Because your health inputs are processed entirely in your browser and never transmitted to our servers, the risk of a data breach involving your health information is effectively zero — we simply don't have it. For any contact information we do receive (such as emails), we take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorized access.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of SageMyHealth after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, or if you'd like to exercise your privacy rights, please get in touch:

Email: hello@sagemyhealth.com

We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 5 business days.
